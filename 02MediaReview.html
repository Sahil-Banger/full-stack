The Knight Review

Adventure

The Knight is a delightful, heartwarming adventure that perfectly blends vibrant animation with a touching story about courage and friendship. Kids will absolutely love the goofy dragon sidekick, while parents will appreciate the film's beautiful message about staying true to yourself. It is an absolute must-watch for your next family movie night!

Packed with colorful visuals and non-stop laughs, The Knight is a magical ride that will keep young audiences completely spellbound from start to finish. It is a charming, fast-paced tale of bravery filled with funny talking animals and epic knight battles.

Pros:

  1. Stunning Animation
  2. Hilarious Sidekicks
  3. A Positive Message

Cons:

  • Predictable Plot
  • Weak Villain
  • Pacing Issues
